# Narrative intent (historical design notes)

These are the original story and implementation passes. For the current state,
use [DIRECTION.md](DIRECTION.md) and the live [TODO.md](../TODO.md).

Current execution plan: [finish the run](docs/DIRECTION.md). The passes below
describe the narrative intent; wrong prints, return events, and last-page ending
variations already exist. Follow the current plan for remaining integration work.

The game already has a morning that will not settle, and a run that does not need it. The pages disagree about who waved, whose strap it is, whether the mat moved, and whether the lights at the end are a car. The house disagrees with the pages: a candle burned down on the stand, a pack candle that was never lit, a mat that turns the first time she comes back from the snow. The things in the field are not people. The figure is a rule about being still and being seen. The Listener is a rule about breath. The sleeper's past is the gap where a name would be.

Play does not yet belong to that argument. After the third trail note the correct action is to keep moving, and the road is always the way out. Reading is a risk because it holds her still, not because the page changes what the snow will say about her. The Listener and the figure can both end the run, and neither ending knows about the other, or about the card on the mortuary desk. The body under the sheet, the height that is neither measurement in the file, and the initial that is not Mara never have to meet the thing she is fleeing.

Elevate the idea, not the cast. She is writing a record with her feet that will not match the record she is reading. Leaving is abandoning an unfinished line. Stopping is letting the snow close a count. Nobody on the pages gets to say which of those she did.

## Keep

- Five trail notes. They already contradict. A sixth note would explain them, and it would start the walk early.
- Mara, the writer, and the sleeper stay unresolved. No page states who is who. The body downstairs stays unnamed, and it still appears only after she has left the mortuary and come back.
- The figure and the Listener stay rules. No origin, no human past, no hint that either of them is someone from the notes.
- Endings stay short. The catch is still pressed snow and nothing leading away. The road is still headlights, and it still does not admit that the last page was lying.
- The house is not a fourth creature. It keeps her where the one on the step can wait.

## The debt

Three records are already in the game, and only one of them is played.

1. **The pages.** A hand that needs the line finished, and that claims the strap. Standing still to read is already its trap.
2. **The file.** A height, a procedure, a breath that was logged. The Listener enforces the breath and does not care about the name.
3. **The snow.** Prints that were counted twice and did not match. A mat that was straight. Her boots now press a bowl and leave a berm. That mark is still only a trace on the ground. Nothing in the run is allowed to be wrong about it.

The elevation is to let these three fail to agree in front of her, the way the notes already fail to agree on the table.

## Passes

Each pass should make one disagreement show up in what she does. Do not add a system that tells her she noticed.

### 1. The snow answers back

Her prints are the only account she makes herself. Use that.

Looking back along a stretch she has already walked should sometimes be wrong by one. A print she did not make, or one of hers already gone. Never a counter, never a prompt. The second trail page already taught the rule: the second count had one more, and it stopped at the mat.

The mat is the first of these, and it only happens because she left and returned. The fourth page says the writer went back and the mat was still straight. One further disagreement of that kind is enough. Prefer the snow outside the door to a new object indoors.

The bowl under the boot is the packed account. The berm is the snow that left. When a print is "wrong," prefer a mark that could not have been her stride: both feet, or a sole with no berm, or a berm with no bowl. Do not spawn a second character to have made it.

1. Add a kind on the existing print, not a new mesh. Hers stays the bowl plus the berm. The wrong ones are a sole with no berm, a berm with no bowl, or both feet side by side. Same shader, same pool.
2. Once per run, after she has left a stretch of her own prints and the stretch is behind her, either settle one of hers early or lay one wrong mark in it. Not while she is looking at that ground.
3. After the mat has turned, one mark in the snow just outside the door. Either a sole with no berm, or one of hers beside a mat the page said was still straight. Not both, and not a new prop.
4. Play it. Walk out, come back, and look at the step. The wrong mark should be obvious as snow and not obvious as a system.

### 2. The two rules are the two debts

She is always paying one of them.

Standing to read feeds the writer and the figure. The hunt already keeps walking while the page is open. What it does not do is care which page. The last page is the one that asks her to finish. Being caught while that page is open should be the same ending as any other catch, with one difference of debt: the line was not finished. Same snow. The card does not explain this.

Running and panting feeds the sleeper's body and the Listener. Being heard should stay a death about breath, not about identity. If she has read the intake card, the death does not get to use the initial on it.

They should not both be loud at once. Whichever pressure is stronger already speaks on the glass. Extend that courtesy to the field: the Listener does not hunt well while the figure is the thing she is fleeing, and the figure does not close while she is spending everything on not breathing. One rule in the foreground. The other waits.

1. Remember whether the page open at the catch is the last trail note. Only then, one extra short sentence on the catch card: the line was not finished. Any other catch stays the pressed snow.
2. Leave the Listener's card as a death about breath. If she has read the intake, do not put that initial, or any name, on the card.
3. While the figure is pursuing, the Listener stays still or drifting and does not hunt. While she is holding her breath and the Listener is the stronger pressure, the figure does not close the gap into a catch. One of them in the foreground.
4. Play both. Read the last page and get caught. Sprint, pant, and get heard. Then do each while the other rule is the loud one, and confirm the quiet one waits.

### 3. The house is where the accounts overlap

The rooms already have the pieces. The pass is order, not furniture.

She wakes on the sleeper's side of the story: a lantern she did not light, wool that is warm, a page that does not know her name.

The ground floor is the writer's side: the candle that burned, the door, the mat, the three strikes.

The cellar is the file's side: a length, an initial, a body that was not there the first time. Nothing down there should confirm Mara, and nothing should deny her in a sentence. The card and the sheet are enough.

Coming back upstairs should be the moment the house has chosen a version. The lantern, the mat, and the body already do this. Do not add a fourth trick. Make sure those three cannot all be true of the same person, and that she can see two of them on one return.

1. Walk one return before changing anything. Leave the bedroom, come back from the snow, go down and come up. Note which of the lantern, the turned mat, and the sheeted body can be true at the same time.
2. If two of them cannot be seen on that return, fix timing or where she stands, not the set of events. No fourth haunt.
3. Check the three against one person. The lantern was lit for the sleeper. The mat moved, which the writer said did not happen. The sheet is a length and an initial that are not the pack. Leave any sentence that would settle it out of the card and the pages.
4. Play the return again. She should be able to see two without a prompt.

### 4. The road is a choice of record

The headlights stay real. The last page stays a plea, not a confession.

If she has not read the last page, the escape card is only the road.

If she has, it is the same road, and the line was left unfinished. One short sentence, still in the writer's voice, still not an explanation. The catch card, if it happens on that page, belongs to the snow instead: the count closed.

Do not add a third ending that tells her who she was. Do not make the Listener's death a revelation about the body.

1. Branch the escape card on whether that last trail note was collected, not on how many notes she has.
2. Write the one extra sentence in the writer's voice. Keep the headlights. Do not say the page was wrong.
3. If the catch happens with that page open, the snow's sentence replaces the writer's. One line. The count closed. Not a name.
4. Play three endings: leave without the last page, leave after it, and be caught while it is open. The first is only the road. The other two are the same events with a debt attached.

## Sourcing

Do not build a mesh for this. The disagreement is a mark, a sentence, or a rule that waits.

- Prints, the wrong ones included, stay the footprint patch already in the game. A kind on that shader. No new sole model, no decal texture, no second character to have walked there.
- The mat, the burned stub, the nightstand page, and the intake card are already placed. Another object only if a return cannot show two of the existing three, and then only by moving one of those, not by adding a fourth.
- If something outdoors is unavoidable, take it from `assets/environment/` or the inventory already on disk, the same way the house took Poly Haven. A missing mesh stays a box. Do not author a new sculpt, and do not bring in a character pack for a print.
- The figure and the Listener stay the Quaternius male rig already in `addons/quaternius_ik_rigged/`. She stays the Styloo elf. No other body, even from a pack that is already downloaded. Those rigs do not share this walk, and a new silhouette would be a new person.

## Animation

No new clip. The library already says who moves like a person and who does not.

- Her steps stay `FootLock` on the elf, with `Stride` playing `Walk_Formal`, `Jog_Fwd`, and `Sprint` from `elf_animations.res`. A wrong print is laid when she is not looking. It is not an extra foot in her cycle, and it does not need a retarget.
- Reading stays the phase that holds her still. Do not give her a writing pose or a wave. Either gesture would decide the pages.
- The figure keeps the clips it has. Still, and watched, it is `Idle`. Once it is pursuing it is `Walk` or `Sprint` on the same Quaternius source. Waiting at the doorstep stays `Idle`. The knock is the sound, not a fist.
- The Listener keeps a paused idle on that same rig. It glides. It does not walk, and it does not get a listen or a kill clip. Breath is `Breath.plume`, hidden when she holds it.
- Rebuild `elf_animations.res` with `tools/retarget_elf.gd` only if a clip she already uses breaks. Do not bake a new one for this plan.
- The end cards are text. She is already stopped. Nothing plays under them.

## Stop

When a player can finish the run and honestly not know whether the woman ahead, the hand on the page, and the woman who woke are one person, and can also point to one thing the snow did that none of those three would have written down, the concept is high enough. More notes, a name for the body, or a reason for the figure would lower it.
