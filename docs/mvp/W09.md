# W09 — red thread

Implemented 2026-10-17 and verified through `tools/thread_probe.tscn`. This package
is part of G3; W10 (storm lamp) and W11 (chapter connections) follow.

## What plays

Latching the storm-loosened window in the house reveals red wool caught in the frame,
opening the red-thread walk. Following the wool leads outside into the trees along
the route's wooded shoulder across five distinct snags:

1. **Window frame:** First snag appears when the window is latched.
2. **Pine branch:** Snag 2 is hidden under heavy snow on a low branch; knocking
   the snow loose with interact or an arrow reveals the thread.
3. **Midway snag:** Leads further into the treeline along natural sightlines.
4. **Forest snag:** Winding the wool loose guides the player toward an inscribed birch.
5. **Carved birch & initials:** At the birch tree, snow banks over their carved initials.
   Brushing the snow clears the bark and reveals Mathilda's red glove beneath.

Taking the glove awards the keepsake **The thread** to the journal. In accordance
with decision D10, the keepsake has `counts = false` and `lake_counts = false`:
it is preserved in the journal as an errand token but does not satisfy or modify
the three-entry minimum for the lake ending or meeting prerequisites.

The glove can be carried, set down safely in the snow, or placed on the wooden crate
at Mathilda's camp. Dropping the glove in the snow and picking it back up does not
duplicate the keepsake. Placements use the standard Errands and Carryable registry
and are stable across save/load cycles.
