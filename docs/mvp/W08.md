# W08 — two cups

Implemented 2026-10-15 and verified after the placement pass on 2026-10-16,
on the W07 warm-house work. This package starts G3; W09–W11 still remain.
It ships with W07 and the indoor lighting pass in v0.2.17.0.

## What plays

Opening the cupboard above the right kitchen counter reveals one cup and the
ring left by the missing one. The errand enters the journal's Undone list.
Mathilda's cup rests half under snow on a short rail at the east edge of the
front landing. Taking it clears its snow. Both cups are carried separately
to staggered places by their chairs on the kitchen table, each at a different
angle. A cup keeps its own place even if the other is taken away or they are
put down in the opposite order. The kettle starts beside the sink rather
than directly under the cupboard; it rests at a casual angle on the counter
and stove. The porch cup is off-centre on its rail, with snow banked around
one side. These placements are authored and stable through saves. This
placement pass follows the owner's 2026-10-16 request for a more natural,
organic arrangement. The old decorative kitchen tea set is hidden; the
reading-room tea set remains.

Carry the kettle from the counter onto the stove. It may sit there cold, but
warming requires both cups on the table and the completed warm-house errand
with its stove actually lit. Hold interact on the kettle to finish. Soft steam
shows the warmth; the existing licensed kettle sound is a placement clank,
not a boiling loop. Once complete, the cups and kettle stay put so the lake
line remains true. The lower fire door still offers warming her hands.

The cupboard, rail, items and sockets live under Errands/World. Stable ids
use the existing Errands/RunSave registry. No errands are built in Mathilda's
chapter or the dream. The cups and jar reuse the already-curated Leartes
models from `build/vendor/requested_errands/leartes/`; their source paths,
hashes and local-use restriction remain in the existing errands manifest.
The jar gains a kettle lid, handle and spout. Without the pack, recognisable
built cups and kettle replace them. Models are fitted to metre-scale bounds;
no proprietary files are added to tracked assets.

## Integration fixes found while verifying

- A taken cup must be released from the cupboard's visibility ownership;
  closing or restoring the cupboard must not hide it after it is carried,
  dropped or placed elsewhere. `HouseDrawer.release` and saved ownership
  handle this without replaying discovery or pickup on load.
- The imported stove's actual top is lower than its old 1.2 m blocker. The
  kettle now follows the top mesh after the editable level is applied, and
  a private collision shape matches that height. The fallback uses its own
  top. No authored scene or shared collision resource is rewritten.
- The old hand-warming target covered the stovetop. Its stove-only target
  now covers the lower fire door, with the campfire target unchanged.
- The second decorative tea set receives an automatic duplicate node name.
  Replacement therefore identifies its source scene as well as its name
  and kitchen location.
- The cupboard was moved clear of the window and sink tap after inspecting
  the rendered scene. Snow and steam were revised after that same review.
