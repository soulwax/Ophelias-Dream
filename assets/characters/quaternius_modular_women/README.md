# Quaternius modular women

These GLBs are from Quaternius's Ultimate Modular
Women pack. They were downloaded in GLB format from the [Poly Pizza pack page](https://poly.pizza/bundle/Ultimate-Modular-Women-Pack-aCBDXDdTNN).
The [official pack page](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html)
describes ten characters with 24 animations. The selected models have a
62-joint humanoid rig and 24 embedded animations each. The four CC0 models are
Adventurer, Hooded Adventurer, and two Animated Woman variants. Two additional
neutral models, Worker and Suit, are marked Creative Commons Attribution on
their [Poly Pizza pages](https://poly.pizza/m/E8079Ahx7k) and
[Suit page](https://poly.pizza/m/sOUciDsoVV).
